Install without a Mac

One-time bootstrap: we sign only DefianceSign.ipa with your certificate, then delete your files from the server.

1. Upload.p12 + profile
2. SignMatches your App ID
3. InstallSafari on iOS
Safari on iPhone or iPad. Use any iOS Development or Ad Hoc certificate and profile with your device UDID. We re-sign DefianceSign to your App ID automatically. Files are destroyed after signing.
  • After install, import the same certificate into DefianceSign to sign other IPAs locally.
  • Development certificates expire after 7 days — use this page again to re-install.
  • DefianceSign cannot update itself; use this page or Sideloadly for updates.
