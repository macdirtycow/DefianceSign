Sign an IPA in the browser

Upload your .p12, .mobileprovision, and optionally any .ipa (TrollStore, AltStore, ESign, another package manager, …). We sign it with your certificate, then delete your files.

1. Upload.p12 + profile + IPA
2. SignMatches your App ID
3. Installor download signed IPA
Works from Safari on iPhone/iPad, or from a Mac. Leave the IPA field empty to sign DefianceSign itself. Upload another IPA to sign that instead (max 400 MB). Use a Development or Ad Hoc profile with your device UDID.
  • On a Mac: download the signed IPA and install it with Sideloadly, AltStore, SideStore, or ESign.
  • On iPhone/iPad: tap Install in Safari, then Trust the developer profile in Settings.
  • A specific App ID profile rewrites the app’s bundle ID to match. Wildcard profiles keep the original ID if they cover it.
  • Development certificates expire after about 7 days — sign again here when the app stops opening.
“Integrity could not be verified”?
  • Use an iOS App Development or Ad Hoc profile — not App Store Distribution.
  • Your iPhone/iPad UDID must be in the profile (register it in Apple Developer → Devices).
  • Prefer a specific App ID profile, or a wildcard that covers the app’s bundle ID.
  • After install: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Trust your developer profile.
  • Turn off VPN, use automatic date/time, and try DNS 8.8.8.8 if verification keeps failing.
