Why DefianceSign

Your keys, your device

Certificates and passwords never leave your iPhone or iPad. No backend, no shared certs.

App Library

Browse community sources from Ksign, Feather, SideStore, and more — optional, on your terms.

Full signing pipeline

Quick start

01
Install DefianceSign

Sideload the IPA with Sideloadly, ESign, or SideStore using your developer certificate.

02
Import your certificate

Settings → Certificates → import .p12 and .mobileprovision. Bundle ID must match your App ID.

03
Sign & install

Add an IPA, sign it, tap Install. Trust your developer profile in iOS Settings if prompted.
